AI & Agentic Engineering

Applying AI to real engineering problems, with architecture and human control intact.

AI-assisted engineering is most valuable when it goes beyond code generation. I use agentic AI for understanding existing systems, analysing requirements and architecture, planning changes, implementing and reviewing code, troubleshooting complex problems, documenting decisions and validating results.

Governed AI-assisted development

My practical work includes extensive use of IBM Bob as an AI development partner, complemented by Microsoft Copilot as an independent reviewer. The approach emphasizes repository-grounded analysis, requirements traceability, explicit approval gates, source verification and recorded build and test evidence.

The objective is not autonomous code generation. It is faster engineering with stronger context, review and evidence.

AI provides acceleration. Architecture, evidence and human accountability provide control.
